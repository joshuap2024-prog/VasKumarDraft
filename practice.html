Practice Areas

Legal support across disputes and business

The firm's work is built around litigation, dispute resolution, and the corporate and business advisory support that clients need before, during, and after a dispute or commercial decision.

01

Practice Area

Litigation

Civil litigation is at the core of the firm's practice. The firm handles contested matters in the Singapore courts, managing every stage of the process from pre-action correspondence through to hearings, judgments, and enforcement.

Good litigation is built on preparation. The firm takes time to understand the facts, identify the legal issues, assess the strength of the claim or defence, and develop a strategy that keeps the client's practical objective in view throughout.

  • Civil claims and defences in the General and Magistrate's Courts
  • Commercial and contractual disputes
  • Injunctions, interim orders, and urgent applications
  • Enforcement of judgments and court orders
  • Pre-action correspondence and demand letters
  • Appeals and post-judgment applications
02

Practice Area

Dispute Resolution

Not every dispute needs to go to court. Many matters are better resolved — more quickly, more privately, and with less cost — through structured negotiation, mediation, or other resolution processes.

The firm supports clients through the full range of dispute resolution options, helping them assess which route best serves their interests and representing them through whichever process is chosen.

  • Negotiation and settlement correspondence
  • Mediation at the Singapore Mediation Centre (SMC)
  • Without prejudice settlement discussions
  • Resolution of commercial, contractual, and business disputes
  • Post-settlement documentation and discharge
03

Practice Area

Corporate Advisory

Corporate decisions carry legal consequences that are not always visible at the time. The firm provides practical advisory support to help businesses understand those consequences before they commit — and to address them clearly when issues arise.

The advice is commercially grounded. The aim is not to outline every theoretical risk, but to identify what matters in the context of what the client is actually trying to do.

  • Corporate governance and directors' duties
  • Shareholders' agreements and shareholder disputes
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and business sales
  • Due diligence support and legal risk assessment
  • Regulatory compliance and ACRA obligations
  • Advice on restructuring, winding up, and insolvency
04

Practice Area

Business Advisory

Business owners and management teams regularly face decisions with legal dimensions — a new agreement, a difficult customer, a dispute with a supplier, a question about what the contract actually allows. The firm provides straightforward guidance on those questions so that decisions are made with a clear view of the legal position.

The approach is practical. Legal advice that cannot be used is not useful advice.

  • Contract review and negotiation support
  • Advice on commercial relationships and agreements
  • Legal risk identification for business decisions
  • Guidance on obligations to employees, partners, and third parties
  • Pre-dispute assessment and early legal intervention
05

Practice Area

Commercial Documentation

Legal documents carry weight long after they are signed. A poorly drafted agreement, a vague clause, or an incomplete settlement can create problems that are expensive and time-consuming to fix. The firm prepares and reviews commercial documents with the care that accurate documentation requires.

Work in this area spans the full lifecycle of a business relationship — from the initial agreement to final settlement and discharge.

  • Drafting and review of commercial agreements and contracts
  • Service agreements, supply agreements, and partnership documents
  • Settlement agreements and deed of releases
  • Letters of demand and formal legal correspondence
  • Board resolutions and corporate records
  • Memoranda of understanding and heads of agreement
06

Practice Area

Risk & Compliance

Legal risk is easier and less expensive to address before a dispute begins than after. The firm helps clients identify exposure in their agreements, operations, and internal processes, and put in place practical steps to reduce it.

This work supports better decision-making and, where issues have already emerged, provides a clearer picture of the client's position before the matter escalates.

  • Legal risk assessment for business operations and decisions
  • Review of internal processes and contractual arrangements
  • Regulatory obligations and compliance matters
  • Identification of liability exposure before transactions
  • Advice on dispute avoidance and pre-litigation options
