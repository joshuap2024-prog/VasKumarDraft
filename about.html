About The Firm

A firm built on careful counsel

Vas Kumar & Co. is a Singapore law firm providing clear, practical legal support to individuals and businesses in contested matters and advisory work. The firm's approach is grounded in direct communication, careful preparation, and steady representation.

The Firm

Focused on what matters most

Vas Kumar & Co. is structured to serve clients who need reliable legal guidance in disputes, commercial matters, and business decisions. The firm operates with a direct working style — no unnecessary layers, no vague advice.

Clients receive clear information about their legal position, the options available to them, and what each path involves in practical terms. That clarity is the foundation of every engagement.

The firm's work spans civil litigation, dispute resolution, corporate and business advisory, commercial documentation, and risk and compliance support. Clients include individuals, small and medium businesses, and corporate entities in Singapore and the region.

Principal

Vas Kumar

Mr Vas Kumar is an advocate and solicitor of the Supreme Court of Singapore. He has extensive experience in civil litigation and dispute resolution, and advises clients across a range of commercial and corporate matters.

His approach is to understand the practical objective behind every legal matter, identify the best path toward it, and represent the client's position with care and discipline from start to finish.

How We Work

Direct engagement from day one

The firm operates without unnecessary distance between the client and the work being done. You deal directly with the principal, and you are kept informed at every stage of your matter.

01

Initial Consultation

The first step is understanding your matter — the facts, the documents, the other parties involved, and what you are trying to achieve. This gives the firm what it needs to give you an honest assessment.

02

Clear Advice

After reviewing the matter, you receive a plain-language view of your legal position, the options available, the realistic risks, and the recommended next steps. Nothing is withheld to protect the relationship.

03

Careful Execution

When you decide on a course of action, the firm carries it out with precision — whether that means filing, drafting, negotiating, or representing you in a dispute. Progress is communicated regularly.
